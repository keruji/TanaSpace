# TanaSpace

**A place for the things you own and the plans you’re making.**

It’s easy for everyday details to end up scattered across notes, lists, and
spreadsheets. TanaSpace is a project working toward one personal
space to keep those details together—from what’s in your pantry to what you
want to build next.

## What you can do today

TanaSpace is in early development. You can currently:

- Explore the homepage to learn about the project.
- Open a private demo space for one hour from the homepage.
- Organize items with required Tana Types, and view, edit, or delete them in My Tana.
- Add, edit, and delete types; a type in use cannot be deleted until its items are moved or removed.
- Save up to 20 types and 100 items per account.
- See your latest items on the dashboard.
- See when you joined and your latest sign-in time.
- Log out to remove your demo space immediately. Expired demo accounts and data are cleaned automatically.

The dashboard is the starting point for your space. My Tana holds the items
you save. Favorites, search, and notifications are visible as previews, but
aren’t available to use yet. More item details and planning tools are still
to come.

## What’s coming

The goal is to help you organize the things you own, use, and plan:

| Planned feature | How it could help |
| --- | --- |
| Richer item records | Add more details about your devices, tools, supplies, and other belongings. |
| Expiration and warranty reminders | Remember what needs using, renewing, or checking before a deadline. |
| Grocery checklists | Plan shopping trips with lists you can reuse. |
| Recipe collections | Save ingredients, instructions, and preparation notes in one place. |
| PC build planning | Keep track of parts and ideas for your next computer or upgrade. |
| Favorites | Return to the records you use most often. |

These features are planned; release dates haven’t been set.

## Follow the project

TanaSpace is growing one step at a time. You can follow this repository for
updates or use its issue tracker to share ideas and report problems.
