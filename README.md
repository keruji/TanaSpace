<h1 align="center">TanaSpace</h1>

<p align="center">
  <strong>A place for the things you own and the plans you’re making.</strong>

Keep everyday details together instead of scattering them across notes, lists,
and spreadsheets. TanaSpace gives you a personal space to organize your
belongings, save useful details, and find your recent records.

## Features

### Keep track of your things

Save devices, tools, supplies, and other belongings in **My Tana**. Give each
item a name and type, add a description and notes, and update its details
whenever something changes. View, edit, or remove records from one place.

### Organize your collection your way

Use **Tana Types** to group your records into categories that make sense to
you, such as electronics, kitchen supplies, or garden tools. Add descriptions
and choose the order of your types to keep your collection easy to browse.

### Pick up where you left off

Your **dashboard** brings your latest items together, so you can return to
recent records or add something new. Switch between light and dark themes;
your choice carries across the site and is remembered for your next visit.

## Find your way around

1. [Open TanaSpace](https://tanaspace.dpdns.org).
2. Go to **Tana Types** and create a category for your items.
3. Open **My Tana**, choose **Add item**, and save your first record.
4. Return to the **Dashboard** to see your latest items.
